# Privacy Policy: SEO Skills AI

SEO Skills AI is designed with strict **privacy-by-design** principles:

1. **Zero Tracking / Zero Analytics:** No cookies, no session telemetry, no tracking pixels.
2. **Local Machine Execution:** Audits, SQLite baselines, and reports remain 100% on your local filesystem.
3. **No Key Sharing:** Third-party credentials remain strictly within your local `~/.config/seoskillsai/` directory.
