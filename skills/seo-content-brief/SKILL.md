# Semantic Content Brief Generator

Generates a production-ready blueprint for copywriters and AI agents before article drafting.

## 📋 Content Brief Specification

Each brief contains:
1. **Target Query & Modality:** Primary keyword, search volume, intent (informational, commercial, navigational), and opening paragraph modality.
2. **EAV Entity Taxonomy:** Central Entity, 5 Secondary Entities, and 12 LSI semantic attributes.
3. **Structured Heading Outline:** Complete `H1`, `H2`, and `H3` architecture enforcing the structured educational prose standard.
4. **Internal Link Requirements:**
   - 2 upward links to Silo Hub and Root Pillar.
   - 2 horizontal links to companion cluster nodes.
   - Exact anchor text assignments obeying the $\le 3$ sitewide limit.
5. **Schema.org Graph Spec:** Exact JSON-LD types to embed (`TechArticle`, `SoftwareApplication`, `BreadcrumbList`). Do not add `FAQPage` for commercial rich results.
