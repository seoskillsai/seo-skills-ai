# Helpful Content Fluff Removal & Humanizer Reference

Aligned with Google Quality Rater Guidelines (QRG) §4.6.5 and §4.6.6.

---

## 🚫 AI Fluff Patterns to Eliminate

1. **Empty Introductory Preamble:**
   - *Example to avoid:* "In today's fast-paced digital world, search engine optimization is more important than ever..."
   - *Fix:* Start directly with the answer: "Google Information Gain Patent US 11,562,019 B2 rewards content with unique empirical data points."
2. **Generic Formulaic Conclusions:**
   - *Example to avoid:* "In conclusion, by following these tips, you will take your SEO to the next level."
   - *Fix:* Replace with a structured summary table, code snippet, or next actionable step.
3. **Low-Information Repetitive Restatements:**
   - Eliminate filler sentences that merely restate headings in different words.
