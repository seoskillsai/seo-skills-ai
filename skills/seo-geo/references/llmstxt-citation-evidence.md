## 🔍 1. Primary Empirical Findings

1. **Self-Contained Answer Passages:**
   - AI retrieval engines extract answer blocks between **130 and 170 words** that directly resolve user intent without requiring surrounding contextual references.
2. **`llms.txt` Standard Adoption:**
   - Adopted across leading AI platforms (Anthropic, OpenAI, Cursor, Google), `/public/llms.txt` provides clean markdown documentation maps to prevent hallucinated endpoints.
3. **Entity Knowledge Graph Triples:**
   - Direct entity linkages (via Schema.org `sameAs` to Wikidata and Wikipedia) increase entity recognition confidence in neural search embeddings.
