# Google Business Profile (GBP) Deprecation & Compliance Reference

Guidelines for avoiding retired GBP features and doorway page penalties.

---

## 🚫 Retired GBP Features

1. **`.business.site` Websites:**
   - Retired March 2024. All traffic redirected to main business domain. Replace any legacy links with your primary website URL.
2. **Direct Chat Field Integration:**
   - Retired July 2024. Direct in-profile messaging moved to SMS / third-party CRM hooks.
3. **Multi-Location Doorway Page Thresholds:**
   - Warning at 30 city-specific landing pages; hard stop at 50 pages unless each page contains unique location photos, local staff bios, and authentic local reviews.
