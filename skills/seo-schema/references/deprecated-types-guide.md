# Deprecated Schema.org Types Guide (2023–2026)

This reference documents schemas that search engines have retired, deprecated, or restricted.

---

## 🚫 Deprecated Types Catalog

1. **`HowTo`**:
   - Retired September 2023. Rich results removed globally on desktop and mobile.
   - **Migration:** Convert to structured `TechArticle` or `Article` with ordered step lists.

2. **`SpecialAnnouncement`**:
   - Retired July 2025.
   - **Migration:** Convert to standard `WebPage` or banner elements.

3. **`ClaimReview`**:
   - Retired June 2025 for general publishers.
   - **Migration:** Ground claims with direct external citations in body prose.

4. **`VehicleListing` / `EstimatedSalary` / `LearningVideo` / `CourseInfo Carousel`**:
   - Retired June 2025 across all regional search engines.

5. **`FAQPage` (Commercial Snippet Deprecation)**:
   - Restricted in August 2023; rich snippet display discontinued for commercial sites in May 2026. Keep for internal semantic knowledge graph extraction only.
