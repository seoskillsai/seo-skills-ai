# 2026 E-E-A-T & Google Quality Rater Guidelines (QRG) Rubric

Evaluation matrix based on the latest September 2025/2026 Quality Rater Guidelines updates.

---

## 🏛️ The 4 E-E-A-T Dimensions

1. **Experience (First-Hand Empirical Evidence):**
   - Direct screenshots, original empirical test results, unique audio/video, or personal workflow case studies.
2. **Expertise (Topical & Technical Depth):**
   - High semantic attribute coverage (EAV modeling), proper technical terminology, and verifiable author credentials.
3. **Authoritativeness (External Consensus & Citation):**
   - Grounding in authoritative industry standards, external citations, and natural backlink profiles.
4. **Trustworthiness (Transparency & Security):**
   - Clear First-Exposure AI Editorial Disclosures, HTTPS security, transparent contact details, and explicit correction policies.
